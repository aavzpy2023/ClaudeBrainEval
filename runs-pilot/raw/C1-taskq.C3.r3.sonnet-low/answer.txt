`taskq list --limit N` now works with and without `--json`, and all 28 tests pass (25 existing plus 3 new).

- **Ordering:** it shows at most N tasks in insertion order (by id), not priority order. The new tests check this by adding the third task with the most urgent priority and confirming it still comes last.
- **Usage errors:** a value below 1 (`0`, `-1`) or a non-integer exits with status 2 and an argparse message. The tests cover `0`, `-1` and `x`.
- **Without `--limit`:** `list` behaves as before.

The change is in `taskq/cli.py` and `taskq/repository.py`; `Repository.list_tasks` takes an optional `limit` and applies it in SQL. I haven't committed anything.
