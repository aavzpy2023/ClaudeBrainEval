Yes, it's decided: csvtool does not auto-detect the delimiter. The project notes in `CLAUDE.md` say "`--delim` (default comma, `\t` = tab) applies to input and output; no auto-detection." The `--delim` flag was added in commit `1004441`.

The notes don't say who made this call. The entries are tagged `[auto]`, and no owner is named. The only open item they explicitly say has no owner is the `mean` and empty-cell policy. If you want to revisit auto-detection, you'd need to decide that yourself, since the notes don't record anyone else as the decision-maker.
