Yes. According to the project history, csvtool does not auto-detect the delimiter. That was settled when `--delim` was added. The delimiter defaults to a comma, `\t` means tab, and the flag applies to both input and output.

The history doesn't say who made or owns that decision. It records the decision and the reason (explicit behavior, standard library only), but no named person. I haven't checked the code or commits, so this comes from the session digest only.
