No, it hasn't been decided because the question was settled the other way. The project decision is that `--delim` (default comma, `\t` for tab) applies to both input and output, and there is no auto-detection. The recorded reason is to keep behavior explicit, and no one is named as the owner of that choice.

The decisions that are still open are the final JSON format for `head` (JSON Lines or a single array) and the empty-cell policy that blocks `mean`. The history says neither has an owner.
