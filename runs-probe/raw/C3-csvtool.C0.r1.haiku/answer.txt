I added the `~` operator to `filter`. `name~an` keeps rows where the field contains the value, and the comparison is always case-sensitive text, so `age~3` matches "30" and "35" rather than comparing numbers. `go test ./...` passes.

- **`csvtool_test.go`**: new cases for parsing `name~an`, and for filtering on contains, case-sensitivity (`name~B` matches nothing), and text-not-numeric (`age~3`, `age~1`).
